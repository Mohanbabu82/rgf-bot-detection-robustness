FIGURE 1. Research framework. Two Cresci benchmarks are split into stratified 70/10/20 partitions over five seeds, with all transformations fitted on training rows and test labels masked. Metadata, profile-text and graph features feed tree, graph-neural-network and hybrid baselines and the proposed reliability-gated fusion (RGF) model. Models trained on clean data are evaluated under test-time perturbations and by discrimination, probability-quality, inductive, cross-dataset and statistical analyses.
  files: Fig01_research_framework.pdf/.svg (vector), .png/.tif (600 dpi, 4320x1854 px = 7.20 x 3.09 in)

FIGURE 2. Architecture of RGF. Three experts produce class logits; the text and graph experts are multiplied by their availability flags. A gate network maps six reliability signals (availability flags, scaled log-degree and the gradient-stopped entropy of each expert) to scores, a softmax restricted to the available experts gives the weights, and the fused logits are the weighted sum of expert logits divided by a temperature T fitted on validation data.
  files: Fig02_rgf_architecture.pdf/.svg (vector), .png/.tif (600 dpi, 4320x2274 px = 7.20 x 3.79 in)

FIGURE 3. Training workflow of RGF: per-account modality dropout (p = 0.3; metadata never dropped), fused plus auxiliary expert cross-entropy (λ = 0.5), full-batch AdamW updates, early stopping on validation cross-entropy (patience 40, at most 300 epochs) and temperature selection on the same validation split.
  files: Fig03_training_workflow.pdf/.svg (vector), .png/.tif (600 dpi, 4320x1794 px = 7.20 x 2.99 in)
